=== Tam açıklama EN ===
Hue Hunt is a minimal color perception puzzle.

One box is a slightly different color. Find it before you run out of lives.

• Levels get harder as the grid grows and the color gap shrinks
• Three lives per run — they do not refill when you level up
• Color-blind mode picks pairs a red-green color-blind player can tell apart
• Turkish and English
• Sound and haptics you can turn off
• No account, no ads in this version

Made by ARU SOFT.
